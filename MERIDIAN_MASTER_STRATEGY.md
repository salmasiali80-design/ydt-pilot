**Meridian tam bu boşluğu kapatmak için tasarlandı.**

**Meridian Nedir?**

Meridian; İngilizce sınav hazırlığında öğrenci, öğretmen, veli ve kurum yönetimini aynı performans sistemi içinde buluşturan premium bir dijital eğitim platformudur.

Meridian'ın gücü yalnızca teknolojisinden değil; 25 yılı aşkın öğretmenlik, sınav hazırlığı, öğrenci gözlemi ve ölçme-değerlendirme deneyiminin dijital forma aktarılmasından gelir.

## PART 6 — VOCABULARY MODULE (MERIDIAN LEXICON)

### Module Name
**MERIDIAN Lexicon**  
*Kelime tanımadan sınav performansına.*

### Core Philosophy
> "We do not ask students to memorize words. We train them to recognize, recall, and use academic vocabulary under exam conditions."

### 5-Layer Word Card System
- **Layer 1 — Recognition:** Word + IPA + English definition + Turkish meaning
- **Layer 2 — Natural Meaning:** Daily sentence
- **Layer 3 — Academic/Exam Sentence:** Exam-register sentence
- **Layer 4 — Word Network:** Synonyms + collocations + word family
- **Layer 5 — Exam Trap:** What MERIDIAN does that no one else does

### Structure
- 500+ academic words
- 10-word missions (500 words = 50 missions)
- Spaced repetition: Day 0 → 1 → 3 → 7 → 14 → 30
- Mastery levels: 0 (New) → 1 (Recognized) → 2 (Understood) → 3 (Recalled) → 4 (Used) → 5 (Exam-ready)

### Signature Features
- **Confusion Alert:** affect/effect, economic/economical, historic/historical
- **Word Family Tree:** analyze → analysis → analytical → analytically
- **Collocation Intelligence:** commitment TO, responsible FOR, associated WITH
- **Exam Trap layer on every card**

### Mission Test Structure (per 10 words)
- 5 meaning questions
- 5 sentence-gap questions
- 3 collocation questions
- 3 word-family questions
- 2 confusion questions
- 2 recall questions (Turkish → English)
